Calendar – Privacy

"Calendar" is a private, personal calendar app made for and used by one person. It is not offered to the public.

What it accesses

When its owner signs in with Google, the app can read and change events in the owner's Google Calendar, and read (never send, change or delete) emails in the owner's Gmail, only to find pictures attached to calendar invitations.

What it stores

Calendar settings, icons and event pictures are kept in the owner's own private database and on the owner's own devices. Sign-in tokens are stored in that same private database.

Sharing

Nothing is sold, shared with, or shown to anyone else. Google user data is used only to show the owner their own calendar.
